Experience and use of assets: 18.3 / 45

This is a polished, dark-gold Hyrule page. The official logo is the hero, and the Deku Tree night backdrop crossfades to Link on Epona at dawn when the clock hits zero, which makes a real narrative. There's also a playable five-note ocarina, a calendar file and the visitor's local time. Several things keep it from shipping: Zelda's Lullaby is the wrong melody, phones have no sound toggle (so the ocarina is mute there) and the mobile header collides, the final seconds aren't a designed moment, the ledger promises effects the page doesn't have, and the 'Doomsday Clock' branding and tagline are lifted from the brief. No run content addressed reviewers.

  • Cohesion with the subject: 7/10. Strongly Hyrule-flavoured: Cinzel gold display type, deep forest greens, a Triforce mark in the header, the official logo as the hero centrepiece, the Great Deku Tree as the night backdrop that crossfades to Link on Epona at dawn when the clock hits zero (08, 13), firefly/Navi-like motes, and lore copy ('The Door of Time Is Open', 'welcome back, Hero of Time', 'The Sacred Realm is open'). The five ocarina holes are labelled D F A B D′, the right pitch classes. What holds it back: the famous Zelda's Lullaby is coded and displayed as D F A D F A (Ocarina.tsx:10). Canon is B D′ A twice, so the page's headline melody is wrong, and the release fanfare reuses that same wrong contour (audio.ts:91). Song of Time is given the Sun's Song effect ('the sun and moon trade places'). The forest portrait in card II is labelled 'II · Hyrule Field'. The branding 'Hyrule Doomsday Clock' / 'The Doomsday Clock for' is lifted from the brief's Marvel framing. The red LIVE pill and the coral final-hour cell rims (06, 07) are off-palette. Below the hero the structure is a generic dark landing page (three feature cards, feature panel, timeline list) wearing a Zelda skin.
